Privacy Policy

openclaw-personal · Last updated: 2026-11-05

1. Who operates this app

openclaw-personal is a private OpenClaw AI agent operated by Joohan Lee (MONSTER HOOPS). It is used only by its owner, for the owner's own Google accounts. It is not offered to the public. Contact: monsterhoops.official@gmail.com

2. Google data we access

With the owner's consent, the app requests these read-only OAuth scopes for the owner's own Google accounts (two accounts: a personal account and the MONSTER HOOPS official account):

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly – read Gmail message content, metadata, labels and accessible mail settings, only to respond to the owner's requests
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly – read Google Calendar events
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.readonly – read and download accessible Google Drive files and their metadata (file names and contents) when needed for the owner's request. The app does not modify files
  • openid, email, userinfo.email – identify which Google account was connected

The app never requests write access. It cannot send email, change calendar events, or create, modify or delete Drive files.

3. How we use Google data

Google data is retrieved only when the owner asks the agent a question (for example, searching mail or listing today's events). It is used only to answer that request. Scheduled or automatic access is not enabled; this policy will be updated before any such feature is turned on. We do not use Google data for advertising, profiling, sale, credit decisions, or to build or train our own AI models.

4. How we store Google data

  • OAuth tokens are stored on the owner's own computer, in the operating system keychain and/or a local file with restricted permissions. They are not sent anywhere except to Google to obtain access.
  • Retrieved content (message text, event details, file text) is processed in the agent's working session. The agent's conversation history, which may contain Google data included in prompts and replies, is kept in local session logs on the owner's computer. The agent does not copy Google content into long-term notes unless the owner asks it to.
  • We do not run a server that collects or keeps Google data for other people.

5. Sharing and transfers to third parties

We do not sell Google user data and do not share it with advertisers, data brokers or resellers. Content the agent retrieves may pass through these services only to provide the feature the owner asked for:

  • AI model provider. To answer the owner's request, the app sends the Google data reasonably necessary for that request, as part of the prompt, to Anthropic's Claude (accessed through the owner's own Claude account) for inference. This transfer is made with the owner's authorization solely to provide the requested assistant feature. The app does not use Google data for advertising, profiling, or to train its own models. The provider's retention, training and human-review practices depend on the provider's service terms and on the owner's account privacy settings, which the owner controls. As of 2026-11-05, the owner reports that the account setting allowing conversations to be used to improve the provider's models is turned off.
  • Telegram. Replies are delivered to the owner through a private Telegram chat. Replies may include excerpts or summaries of Google data the owner requested. Messages pass through Telegram's infrastructure and are subject to Telegram's data-handling practices (ordinary Telegram chats are not end-to-end encrypted). The app does not intentionally send Google data to any other chat, group, or recipient.

Other transfers happen only to protect security (for example, investigating abuse) or to comply with applicable law. The operator does not read other people's Google data, and no one other than the owner is given access by the operator. The service providers named above may process the content under their own terms as described.

6. Limited Use

openclaw-personal's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data, and information derived from it, is used only for the owner's authorized, user-facing assistant features, and access and transfers are limited to what those features require.

7. Retention, deletion and revoking access

  • The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • OAuth credentials remain stored locally until they are removed, revoked, or become invalid. Session logs may retain Google data included in prompts and replies. Deleting a session from the active list may not immediately delete compressed local archives or backups on the same computer, so the owner must separately remove active logs, archived sessions, backups and stored credentials.
  • Revoking access in Google Account settings stops further authorized access, but it does not automatically delete data that was already retrieved or copies already transmitted to the service providers named above.
  • Questions or deletion requests: monsterhoops.official@gmail.com

8. Security

Tokens and logs stay on a computer controlled by the owner, access to the agent is limited to the owner's Telegram account, and tool permissions for Google access are restricted to read-only operations.

9. Changes

If the way Google data is used changes, this policy will be updated first and the date above will change.
